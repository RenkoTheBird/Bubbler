WUBBLER COMMUNITY GUIDELINES
Last updated September 10, 2026
‍
Wubbler enables you to build a personalized social media experience by choosing your path and your preferences -- with your unique results. Our Community Guidelines ensure everyone can share content and enjoy it without harming or otherwise hurting others along the way.

These Guidelines are incorporated into our Terms of Service, and further explain what isn’t allowed on Wubbler so that we can help create a positive environment for everyone. Everyone on Wubbler must follow these rules, and they apply to all parts of our platform, including your content and behaviors. 

We review reports by users, moderators, trusted third-party partners, or where required by law. When we proactively or reactively discover a violation of these Guidelines, we may take a number of enforcement steps based on the severity of the violation, including issuing warnings, removing content, temporarily suspending account access to Wubbler, or permanently removing violative accounts, and even reporting them to law enforcement. We may also consider relevant off-platform behavior when making assessments for violations of specific Community Guidelines.

If you come across an account, server, or content that appears to break these Guidelines, please report it to us.

Respect Each Other
1. Do not promote, coordinate, or engage in harassment. We do not allow any type of harassing behavior, including sexual harassment and ban or block evasion.

2. Do not threaten to harm another individual or group of people. This includes direct, indirect, and suggestive threats. 

3. Do not share or threaten to share the personally identifiable information (PII) of an individual without consent. This includes providing services that facilitate doxxing, such as buying or selling doxxes or compiling doxxes for others. 

4. Do not use hate speech or engage in other hateful conduct. This includes the use of hate symbols, imagery, and claims that deny the history of mass human atrocities.

We consider hate speech to be any form of expression that either attacks other people or promotes hatred or violence against them based on their protected characteristics, as defined by United States law.

5. Do not organize, promote, or support violent extremism. This also includes coordinating violent acts; glorifying or promoting violence or the perpetrators of violent acts; and promoting conspiracy theories that could encourage, incite, or promote violence against others. 

6. Do not create, post, solicit, share, or make attempts to distribute content on Wubbler that depicts, promotes, or attempts to normalize child sexual abuse. This includes manipulated, non-manipulated, or synthetic (artificial intelligence-generated) media involving real, fictional, and/or anthropomorphized children. 

This also includes animation, drawing, text, and technical commands or prompts to create media depicting child sexual abuse or child sexualization.

We report child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and grooming to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children, which may subsequently work with local law enforcement. We will cooperate fully with these investigations.

7. Do not share, distribute, or create sexually explicit or sexually suggestive content of other people over the age of 18 without the subject’s explicit consent. This includes the non-consensual distribution of intimate media that was created either with or without an individual’s consent. (See our Non-Consensual Adult Intimate Media Policy Explainer for more.)

8. Do not share content that glorifies, promotes, or normalizes suicide or other acts of physical self-harm. This includes content that encourages others to cut, burn, or starve themselves, as well as content that normalizes eating disorders, such as anorexia and bulimia. Self-harm acts or threats used as a form of emotional manipulation or coercion are also prohibited. 

‍9. Do not upload or share any material depicting violence, gore, or animal cruelty, especially with the intention to harass or shock others. 

Respect Wubbler
10. Do not use self-bots or user-bots. Each account must be associated with a human, not a bot. 

11. Do not engage with our service in an inauthentic way. This includes using bots to artificially inflate engagement, along with selling these services.

12. Do not sell or purchase Wubbler assets, including accounts and usernames.

13. Do not share false or misleading information (otherwise known as misinformation). Content that is false, misleading, and can lead to significant risk of physical or societal harm may not be shared on Wubbler. We may remove content if we reasonably believe its spread could result in damage to physical infrastructure, injury of others, obstruction of participation in civic processes, or the endangerment of public health. 

14. Do not misrepresent your identity on Wubbler in a deceptive or harmful way. This includes creating fake profiles and attempts to impersonate an individual, group, or organization, or to deliberately misrepresent another's identity in a libelous or slanderous manner.

15. Do not evade permanent Wubbler-level enforcement actions. This includes creating new or using existing accounts or communities after being removed for violating our platform rules. 

16. Do not engage in activities that could damage or compromise the security of an account, network, or system. This includes using deceptive techniques to trick others into revealing sensitive information (phishing), using malicious software (malware), and flooding a target with traffic in order to make a resource unavailable (denial-of-service attacks). 

17. Do not use or attempt to use Wubbler to promote, coordinate, or execute financial scams. A financial scam is any intentionally deceptive act taken with the intent to receive an illegal, unethical, or otherwise dishonest gain. 

18. Do not engage in activities that fraudulently generate a profit at the expense of others. This includes facilitating, providing instructions for, and participating in fraud. We do not allow coordinated efforts to defraud businesses, price gouging, forgery, money laundering, counterfeit goods, or tools that facilitate illegal behavior. 

19. Do not submit false, misleading, or abusive reports or requests to Wubbler’s support teams. A report or request will be considered false or misleading if it contains demonstrably untrue information submitted with the intent to manipulate staff into taking a desired action. 

Follow Applicable Laws
As described in our Terms of Service, you must comply with all applicable laws, rules, and regulations. This includes:

20. Do not share content that violates anyone's intellectual property or other rights. 

21. Do not organize, promote, or engage in the selling or facilitation of sales of regulated or potentially dangerous goods. Regulated goods have laws in place that restrict the purchase, sale, trade, or ownership of the goods. Dangerous goods have a reasonable potential to cause real-world, physical harm to individuals. 

22. Do not coordinate or participate in illegal gambling. Users are responsible for complying with applicable gambling laws and regulation. 

23. Do not organize, promote, or engage in any illegal activity that harms the dignity, safety, or wellbeing of another person. This includes but is not limited to human trafficking and sexual solicitation. 

If you see any content or activity that violates these Guidelines, our Terms of Service, or any of our other policies, please report it to us within the app or at support@wubbler.xyz. However, please note that we strongly discourage and may take action against vigilantism, as this behavior can put individuals in harm’s way and can also interfere with our investigations and ability to report to law enforcement.

These Guidelines will continue to evolve over time. This means we may take action against an account, server, or content that violates the spirit of these Guidelines when we encounter a new threat or harm that is not explicitly covered in the current version.

We will always make our best effort to notify you when we update these Guidelines, but it is up to you to follow them and to use Wubbler responsibly: Keep Wubbler safe and help us ensure it remains a great place to build a personal, personalized social media experience. Thanks for doing your part.‍
